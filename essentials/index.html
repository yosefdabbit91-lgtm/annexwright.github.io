Common questions

Is twenty documents too much for a 15-person company?
The Start Here guide's first job is to tell you what to skip. A company using AI tools internally typically needs a handful of them, not all twenty. A company using AI in hiring or lending needs more, and needs a lawyer as well.
Why not just ask an AI model to write these?
You can, and for a single policy you will get something readable quickly. These documents were drafted with AI assistance too — the difference is not human versus machine. It is generated prose versus a structured system: documents that reference each other, controls mapped to clauses you can cite, answers that point at the artifact behind them, registers that calculate rather than describe, and a sequence of work. A general-purpose model asked for "an AI governance policy" produces text, not that architecture, and clause references it produces should be checked against the standard before anyone relies on them.
Can I use it with clients?
No — Essentials and Professional are licensed for one organization's internal use. Consultant is the tier that permits white-label client delivery.
