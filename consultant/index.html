Where the licence line sits

If a client is paying you to help build a governance programme and receives adapted documents as part of that work, that is licensed. Redistributing the templates as templates — reselling them as a document product — is not. That line is the same in every tier.

What you still do. Annexwright supplies the documentation architecture. You decide which systems are in scope, who owns them, what your risk appetite is and what you will actually do; you fill the registers, adapt the wording, operate the controls and keep the records. Buying documentation does not make an organization compliant or certified.

On impartiality. If you audit or certify a client's management system, you should not also supply the documentation they are audited against. This tier is built for the implementation side of that line.
