Straight answers

What this is, and what it isn't.

The honest version, before you buy

Buying documentation does not make an organization compliant. Compliance comes from what you actually do — the systems you register, the risks you assess, the controls you operate, the people you train, the records you keep. This removes the blank page, the research and the structure, which is genuinely most of the drafting work. It does not remove the implementation, and it is not legal advice.

Will this get us certified to ISO/IEC 42001?
It gives you the documentation an auditor expects and the structure to produce the evidence. It cannot give you the records only elapsed time produces — a completed internal audit and a management review. The 90-day roadmap in Professional plans exactly that, and is candid about why first attempts usually fail.
Is this just ChatGPT output?
The file list and the sample above are the test. The Statement of Applicability carries 38 controls with correct identifiers from A.2.2 to A.10.4, each with a purpose, a suggested implementation and an evidence pointer. The risk register holds 41 risks with inherent and residual scoring and a working heat map. Generic AI output does not survive that specificity — which is why the manifest is published rather than hidden behind a form.
We already have ISO/IEC 27001. Is this redundant?
No, and you are the easiest case. Clauses 4–10 are structurally identical, so most of your management system carries over — the free crosswalk shows exactly what transfers. What is genuinely new is AI-specific: impact assessment, data provenance, human oversight, transparency, intended-use control.
We're a 15-person startup. Is this over-engineered for us?
The Start Here guide's first job is to tell you what to skip. A company using AI tools internally needs eight documents, not thirty-five. A company using AI in hiring or lending needs more, and needs a lawyer as well.
How much work is left for us?
All of the company-specific work. You decide which systems are in scope, who owns them, what your risk appetite is, and what you will actually do; you fill the registers, adapt the wording, operate the controls and keep the records. What you do not do is design the document architecture, research the clause mapping or write the first draft.
Can I use this for my clients?
Not on Essentials or Professional — those are licensed for one organization's internal use. The Consultant tier exists for that: rebrand everything and deliver adapted materials to unlimited clients. The one line you cannot cross in any tier is redistributing the templates as templates.
The EU delayed the high-risk rules. Why buy now?
Two obligations are live regardless: Article 4 AI literacy since February 2025, and Article 50 transparency since 2 August 2026, with the marking grace period ending 2 December 2026. The deferral moved Annex III duties to December 2027; it did not cancel them. Meanwhile the thing actually costing companies money today is not a regulator — it is an enterprise buyer's security questionnaire.
There are other ISO 42001 toolkits. Why this one?
There are, and some are good — compare them. Three things separate this one, and all three are checkable rather than claimed. It was built after the Digital Omnibus changed the EU deadlines on 27 July 2026. Its spreadsheets are working models rather than tables printed into a document. And it covers the US state laws that are, for most companies, the nearer deadline. If a competing toolkit does all three, buy theirs.
What format are the files?
Word, Excel and PowerPoint. Fully editable, no watermarks, no locked cells, no PDF-only files. The spreadsheets use standard formulas that work in Excel, Google Sheets and LibreOffice.
